## Restoration emergencies common in Pebble Creek

Hillsborough County's summer pattern of fast-building afternoon thunderstorms and tropical systems drives most of the water and storm calls we see in this part of the county. ## Services we provide in Pebble Creek

Water damage restoration is the call we get most, and in this area it's usually tied to a roof leak during a storm, a slow leak under a slab, or an AC drain line clog rather than a dramatic pipe burst. ## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Pebble Creek

Much of Pebble Creek's housing stock dates to the planned-community development wave of the 1980s and 1990s, built slab-on-grade with concrete block or wood-frame stucco exteriors and shingle or tile roofing, which is standard for Hillsborough County construction of that era. Slab construction means water intrusion tends to travel laterally under flooring before it's visible, and plumbing from this period is typically copper supply with cast iron or early PVC drain lines, both of which have known failure points worth checking when a leak surfaces.

Hillsborough County's sandy, flat terrain drains reasonably well on undisturbed soil but moves water quickly across compacted lots and along foundation perimeters during heavy rain, which is part of why storm-driven leaks here often show up at slab edges and garage thresholds rather than through roof leaks alone.
