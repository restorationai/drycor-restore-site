## Restoration emergencies common in Navarre

Navarre sits on the Santa Rosa Sound side of the Florida Panhandle, and that geography drives most of the calls we get from the area. Tropical systems moving through the Gulf bring wind-driven rain that finds its way under shingles and around window flashing, and storm surge or heavy rain events push water against homes built close to the water on slab foundations. Between storms, the region's high humidity and proximity to brackish water keep moisture pressure on crawlspaces and slab perimeters year-round, which is part of why mold shows up fast after even a small leak goes unnoticed. Summer thunderstorms are frequent and can stall over the area, dumping enough rain in an hour to overwhelm yard drainage and back water up against foundations that were graded decades ago for a drier climate pattern than we see today.

## Services we provide in Navarre

Water damage restoration is our most frequent call in Navarre, and it usually traces back to roof penetrations from wind-driven rain, slab plumbing failures, or washing machine supply lines in homes where galvanized or early-generation plastic piping is reaching the end of its service life. We handle extraction, structural drying tracked to the IICRC S500 standard, and the moisture mapping needed to document the loss for your adjuster.

Storm damage restoration and emergency board-up and tarping go hand in hand here. Mold remediation and mold inspection and testing are steady needs given the humidity and the number of homes with crawlspace or slab-adjacent moisture issues. We scope visible growth and hidden colonization behind walls and under flooring, then remediate to a clearance standard rather than just surface cleaning.

Fire damage restoration and smoke damage restoration address the soot, odor, and structural damage left behind after a kitchen or electrical fire, including content cleaning and storage so salvageable belongings aren't lost to secondary smoke damage during repairs.

Sewage cleanup and sanitization, burst pipe cleanup and repair, water heater flood cleanup, and water leak detection round out the emergency side, while reconstruction services and renovations, remodels, and general contracting carry the property through rebuild once the structure is dry and the scope is finalized.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Navarre

Much of Navarre's housing stock dates from the 1980s onward, with slab-on-grade construction dominating the area and stucco, vinyl, or brick-veneer exteriors over wood-frame walls. Asphalt shingle roofing is common, with a growing share of metal roofing on newer builds, and that roofing age matters directly when wind events loosen fasteners or flashing. Slab construction means water intrusion tends to travel along the perimeter and under flooring rather than down through multiple levels, which changes how a drying plan is scoped.

Plumbing in older Navarre homes may still include galvanized supply lines or early plastic piping nearing replacement age, both of which are common points of failure we plan around during water losses. The sandy coastal soil drains well in most conditions but sits over a comparatively high water table given the area's proximity to Santa Rosa Sound, which can slow how quickly a saturated crawlspace or sub-slab area actually dries. Santa Rosa County issues permits for structural repair and rebuild work in unincorporated Navarre, and flood-zone requirements tied to the area's coastal location often factor into how a damaged structure can be repaired or must be elevated.
