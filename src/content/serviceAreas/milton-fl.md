## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Milton

Much of Milton's housing stock dates from the postwar decades through the 1980s, with slab-on-grade construction dominant and some older homes near the historic core built earlier with crawlspace foundations. Slab homes hide plumbing leaks until water surfaces at baseboards or flooring seams, while crawlspace homes trap moisture against floor joists where it's easy to miss until rot sets in. Plumbing from that era is a mix of copper, galvanized steel, and cast iron drain lines, all of which are past or approaching typical failure age, and homes built before the 1980s may contain asbestos-containing materials or lead-based paint that a restoration scope has to account for before demolition starts.
