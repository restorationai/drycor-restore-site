## Restoration emergencies common in Lake Magdalene

West-central Florida's combination of high humidity, a shallow water table, and a long hurricane season drives most of the restoration calls we see in this part of Hillsborough County. Afternoon thunderstorms stack up through summer and can overwhelm aging drainage around older lots, while tropical systems bring both wind-driven rain and the risk of prolonged power outages that let HVAC systems sit idle, inviting mold growth within 24 to 48 hours of a wet event. Slab-on-grade construction, standard across this area, means a slow plumbing leak or irrigation line break can travel under flooring for weeks before a homeowner notices staining, buckling, or a musty smell. Add in sandy soil and the karst limestone that underlies much of west-central Florida, and even minor grading issues around a foundation can push water toward a structure instead of away from it.
