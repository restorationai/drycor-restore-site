## Restoration emergencies common in Daphne

Daphne sits on the Eastern Shore of Mobile Bay, and that location shapes the kind of calls we get. Hurricane season runs June through November, and even storms that stay offshore push heavy rain bands and straight-line wind across Baldwin County, driving water through roof flashing, soffits, and window seals. The bay's proximity means a high water table in low-lying sections of the city, so crawlspaces and slabs near drainage easements hold moisture longer after a heavy rain event than homes further inland. Combine that with Gulf Coast humidity most of the year and a small plumbing leak that goes unnoticed for a few days can turn into active mold growth well before a homeowner smells anything. Afternoon thunderstorms in summer also bring localized flash flooding on streets that weren't graded for today's rainfall totals, which backs water up against foundations and into garages and ground-floor living space.
