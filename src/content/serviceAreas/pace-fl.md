## Restoration emergencies common in Pace

Pace sits in the Florida Panhandle's humid subtropical belt, which means long, wet summers, frequent afternoon thunderstorms, and a hurricane season that regularly sends tropical systems up through the Gulf. Heavy rain events can overwhelm yard drainage and low-lying lots faster than many homeowners expect, and the region's high humidity gives mold a head start anywhere moisture sits for more than a day or two. Older homes with original plumbing see their share of slow leaks and pipe failures, while newer construction isn't immune to wind-driven rain finding its way behind siding and through roof penetrations during a strong storm.
