## Restoration emergencies common in Lynn Haven

Lynn Haven sits on the North Bay side of St. Andrews Bay in Bay County, which means the restoration calls here follow a predictable pattern: tropical storm and hurricane season pushes wind-driven rain into roof systems and window flashing, storm surge and heavy rain events overwhelm low-lying yards and crawlspaces, and the area's humid, subtropical climate gives any undetected leak a head start on mold growth. A slow roof leak that would take weeks to cause trouble in a drier climate can produce visible mold colonization in Lynn Haven in a matter of days. Homes closer to the bay and to drainage canals also see more frequent minor flooding during heavy rain, even outside of a named storm.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Lynn Haven

Much of Lynn Haven's housing stock reflects a mid-20th-century build-out of concrete block slab-on-grade homes, with a significant wave of roof replacement and rebuilding after Hurricane Michael struck the Panhandle in 2018. That storm history means roofing age and materials vary widely from block to block, and older homes may still carry original cast iron or galvanized plumbing alongside newer PEX repipes done during post-storm repairs. The city's sandy coastal soils and proximity to St. Andrews Bay keep the water table relatively high, which slows drainage around foundations and crawlspaces after heavy rain.
