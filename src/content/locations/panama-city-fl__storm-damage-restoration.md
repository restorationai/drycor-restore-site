## Why Panama City Properties See Storm Damage

The Florida Panhandle takes a direct hit from tropical systems more often than most of the state, and Panama City's position on the Gulf puts it squarely in the path of storm surge pushing up St. Andrew Bay as well as the wind field that follows landfall. Mature pine trees common throughout Bay County are notorious for snapping or uprooting in sustained winds, and a single downed pine can punch through a roof deck, crack a slab foundation, or take down a fence line in seconds. Homes built before the Florida Building Code's post-2004 wind mitigation updates are especially vulnerable, older roof-to-wall connections and original soffit systems don't hold up the way newer construction does. ## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Panama City
