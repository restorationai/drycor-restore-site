## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Lynn Haven

The first call is almost always about water getting in faster than anyone expected, so our crew starts with emergency board-up and tarping to stop the bleeding, then moves into extraction if rain has already soaked flooring, insulation, or drywall. From there we document every affected material with photos and moisture readings before anything gets torn out, because that record is what your adjuster needs to approve the claim without a second visit. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed based on actual moisture readings rather than guesswork, since Florida's humidity means a house that looks dry on the surface can still be holding water in the wall cavity. Once the structure reads dry, we move into reconstruction, whether that's a section of roof decking, drywall, or flooring, so you're working with one crew from the first tarp to the final coat of paint.

## Lynn Haven Insurance & Reconstruction Coordination

Most Lynn Haven storm claims involve wind and water together, which means the adjuster is going to want clear separation between what the wind damaged directly and what followed as water intrusion. We document both categories from day one so the claim doesn't get stuck in the back-and-forth over which peril caused what. For homes in deed-restricted communities, we also coordinate with HOA requirements on exterior repairs, tarp colors, and debris staging, so the fix doesn't turn into a second problem with the property association while you're still dealing with the storm itself.
