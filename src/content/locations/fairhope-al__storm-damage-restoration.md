## Why Fairhope Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Fairhope sits on a bluff above Mobile Bay, which gives the city its views and its vulnerability in the same breath. Storms that track up from the Gulf bring driving rain and wind off open water, and bluff-adjacent homes take a different kind of beating than properties set back from the shoreline, wind-driven rain forced sideways under eaves, soffit damage, and roof uplift at the edges where bay wind gets underneath shingles. Fairhope is also known for its live oak canopy along the historic downtown streets, and those same mature trees that make the city distinctive are the first thing to come down in a tropical system or a straight-line wind event, often onto roofs, fences, and detached garages. Add a hurricane season that runs roughly June through November and a stock of homes that ranges from century-old cottages near downtown to newer construction further inland, and storm response in Fairhope has to account for very different roof ages, framing methods, and wind-rating histories on back-to-back calls.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Fairhope

The first visit is about stopping the bleeding: emergency tarping or board-up on any roof or window breach, followed by a walk of the structure to document wind, water, and debris damage before anything gets moved or cleaned up. Once the property is secured, we extract standing water, pull saturated insulation and drywall that won't dry in place, and set up commercial dehumidification and air movers calibrated to drying per the IICRC S500 standard. Moisture mapping with penetrating and non-penetrating meters tells us where water tracked behind walls or under flooring, which matters more in Fairhope's older homes where plaster and original wood siding hold moisture longer than modern materials. From there, our crews move into reconstruction, whether that's a section of roof decking, exterior siding, or interior finishes, so the property goes from storm-damaged back to livable without a second contractor and a second set of delays.

## Fairhope Insurance & Permitting Coordination

Gulf Coast storm claims almost always involve a wind policy and, separately, a flood policy, and insurers scrutinize the line between the two closely. We document damage by cause, wind-driven rain versus rising water versus storm surge, with photos and moisture readings that give adjusters what they need without guesswork. Baldwin County issues building permits for structural and roofing repairs outside the city's own jurisdiction, and we coordinate that paperwork alongside the insurance claim so repairs don't stall waiting on an inspection that should have been scheduled weeks earlier. As a licensed contractor (#CBC1253966), we handle permitted reconstruction work directly rather than handing it off once the drying equipment comes out.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Storm Damage

Storm jobs mix water mitigation equipment with structural repair gear in a way most other restoration calls don't. We bring industrial extraction units, LGR dehumidifiers, and air movers for the water and humidity side, plus tarping systems, temporary board-up materials, and framing tools for the structural side, often on the same job. Our teams are OSHA trained for the debris handling and fall-protection concerns that come with storm-damaged roofs and downed trees, which matters on a property where a limb has punched through decking and the roof itself isn't safe to walk until it's been assessed.
