**Storm damage, fire, or a break-in left your Fairhope property open to the weather?** We answer 24/7 and dispatch crews to seal roofs, windows, and doors before rain, wildlife, or looters do more damage than the original event. Fairhope's position on the bluff above Mobile Bay means wind-driven rain off the water can turn a cracked window or a tarp-sized roof hole into a soaked interior within hours, and every hour a structure sits open raises the risk of secondary water and mold damage underneath.

## Why Fairhope Properties See Board-Up and Tarping Emergencies

Fairhope sits on the Eastern Shore of Mobile Bay, which puts the area in the direct path of tropical systems moving up from the Gulf during hurricane season, along with the straight-line wind events and pop-up thunderstorms that roll off the bay in summer. Mature live oaks and pecan trees shade much of the older housing stock near downtown, and those same trees drop large limbs on roofs during high wind. Homes built before storm-rated window codes became standard often have original single-pane windows or older roof decking that gives way faster than newer construction, which is why board-up and tarping calls in this area tend to cluster around named storms and the isolated but intense summer squalls that come off the water with little warning.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Fairhope

We start by assessing structural safety before anyone climbs a ladder or walks a damaged roofline, since storm-weakened framing and fire-compromised roof decking carry real fall risk. For broken windows and doors, we measure and cut exterior-grade plywood on site and fasten it to the frame rather than just the trim, which matters on older Baldwin County homes where original wood trim may already be soft from humidity exposure. For roof damage, we use reinforced poly tarps rated for wind and anchor them with batten strips screwed into solid decking, not just staples, because bay-area gusts after a storm can tear loose a poorly secured tarp within a day. Every board-up and tarp job is photographed before and after for the insurance file.

## Fairhope Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most carriers treat board-up and tarping as mitigation work that is expected and reimbursable, since a documented emergency seal helps prevent the "failure to mitigate" disputes that can complicate a larger claim. We photograph the damage and the completed work and provide that documentation for your adjuster. Homes within Fairhope's historic downtown core sometimes fall under design review for visible exterior alterations, so if a board-up will be street-facing on one of those properties, we can keep materials to standard unpainted plywood and tarp colors that typically pass review without issue, and flag anything that may need a longer-term fix once the structure is weathertight.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Board-Up & Tarping

We carry exterior-grade plywood in standard window and door sizes along with sheet stock for larger openings, reinforced tarping rated for sustained wind rather than light-duty poly, and galvanized or stainless fasteners. That fastener choice is not incidental near the bay: salt air accelerates corrosion on standard zinc-coated screws within a season or two, so anything we install that may stay up for more than a few weeks goes in with corrosion-resistant hardware. For fire-damaged structures, board-up also includes securing the opening against weather while the structure is assessed for safe reentry, which we coordinate with the broader fire damage restoration process.

## Local note

Humidity along the bay side of Baldwin County keeps wood doors and window frames slightly swollen most of the year, which means a board that fits snugly in a dry Alabama winter can sit loose by mid-summer. When we board a window or door, we account for that seasonal swelling rather than cutting to a tight winter fit, which keeps the seal tighter through the humid months when storm activity is also at its peak.
