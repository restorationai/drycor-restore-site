## Why Daphne Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of Daphne's housing stock was built on slab foundations, which is standard for the region but means a slow leak under a slab can travel laterally before it ever shows up as a stain on the floor. Older homes closer to the original town center may still have crawl space foundations, where humid Gulf air keeps ambient moisture high even without a leak, so a pipe failure there tends to compound existing dampness rather than introduce a new problem. Add in Baldwin County's hurricane and tropical storm season, and Daphne properties face both wind-driven rain intrusion and the kind of sewer backups that come with a saturated water table pushing groundwater back into lines that would otherwise drain fine. We see a steady mix of slab leaks, water heater failures, and storm-related intrusion depending on the season.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Daphne

The first visit is about stopping the loss and reading the damage, not guessing at it. Our crews use moisture meters and thermal imaging to track water migration through slab-on-grade flooring and behind drywall, which matters in this climate because surface-dry materials can still be holding moisture deeper in the assembly. Extraction comes next, pulling standing water with truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment depending on access, followed by structural drying set up to account for Daphne's ambient humidity, since outdoor air here rarely helps a drying job the way it might in a drier climate. We monitor daily with moisture mapping and adjust airflow and dehumidification until materials hit dry-standard targets, documenting each reading for the insurance file.
