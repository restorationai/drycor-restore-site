**Finding mold in a Carrollwood home after our humid Tampa Bay summers is common, not rare.** Between the afternoon thunderstorms that roll through Hillsborough County from June through September and the central air conditioning systems working overtime to keep up, moisture gets trapped in places homeowners rarely check, closets, attic insulation, the drywall behind kitchen cabinets. By the time a musty smell reaches the living room, colonization has usually been underway for days.

## Why Carrollwood Properties See Mold Issues

Much of Carrollwood was built out during the 1960s through 1980s housing boom, which means a large share of the area's homes are slab-on-grade construction with original plumbing and HVAC runs that have been patched, not replaced, over the decades. Slab homes in this part of Florida are especially prone to moisture wicking up through the concrete when the water table rises after heavy rain, and that moisture migrates into baseboards and drywall long before it's visible. Add in the high ambient humidity typical of unincorporated Hillsborough County, and any undetected roof leak, condensate line clog, or slow plumbing drip becomes a mold problem inside 24 to 48 hours instead of the week or two it might take in a drier climate.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Carrollwood

We start with a moisture map of the affected area using thermal imaging and a hygrometer, because in homes with popcorn ceilings and older drywall it's common for the surface to look dry while the material behind it is still saturated. Once we've confirmed the scope, we isolate the work area with physical containment and negative air pressure so spores don't travel into the rest of the HVAC system, a real risk in single-story homes where one central air handler services the whole footprint. From there, affected materials are removed and bagged per IICRC guidelines, the structure is treated and dried, and we run air scrubbers until moisture readings return to normal for the building type. For jobs involving older homes with lead-based paint layers, our EPA Lead-Safe Certified crew follows the appropriate containment and disposal protocol before any demo begins.

## Carrollwood Insurance & HOA Coordination

A fair number of Carrollwood properties sit inside deed-restricted communities with HOA rules governing exterior dumpster placement, containment signage, and work-crew hours, details that matter when a remediation job runs multiple days. We coordinate scheduling and site setup around those requirements so the job doesn't stall waiting on a committee sign-off. On the insurance side, mold claims tied to a covered water event (a burst supply line, a failed water heater) are usually more straightforward than mold attributed to long-term humidity or deferred maintenance, so we document moisture source and timeline carefully from the first visit to support whichever direction the claim goes.

## Local Note

One thing we watch for specifically in this part of Hillsborough County: homes with mature oak canopy over the roofline hold moisture in the shingles and attic space longer after a storm than homes with full sun exposure, which means attic mold can develop even when the roof itself isn't leaking. If your property has heavy tree cover, it's worth having the attic checked any time you've had sustained rain, not just after a known leak.
