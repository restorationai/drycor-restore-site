## Why Santa Rosa Beach Properties See Plumbing Emergencies

Much of the housing stock along this stretch of the Gulf Coast sits on slab foundations or elevated pilings, and both have their own failure patterns. Slab homes in sandy, well-draining soil can experience minor settling over the years that stresses the copper or PEX runs beneath the concrete, eventually leading to a slow leak that surfaces as a damp carpet or a spike in the water bill before anyone notices a drip. Pilings and crawlspace construction, common in flood-zone coastal builds, expose plumbing fittings directly to salt air, which corrodes brass and steel connections faster than you'd see on an inland property. Add in the number of vacation rentals and seasonal homes in Walton County that sit vacant for stretches at a time, and you get supply lines, wax rings, and water heater connections that fail quietly while no one's there to catch it early.
