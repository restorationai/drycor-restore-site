## Why Panama City Properties See Board-Up and Tarping Needs

Bay County sits on the open Gulf, which means tropical storms, hurricanes, and the occasional straight-line squall line are part of ordinary life here, not rare events. Hurricane Michael in 2018 left a lasting mark on the local roofing stock, and a lot of homes and commercial buildings still carry repairs, patches, or aging shingle systems from that period that are more vulnerable the next time sustained wind or a falling limb finds a weak point. Florida's wind-borne debris requirements exist for a reason: flying shingles, signage, and tree limbs break windows and tear roof decking loose even in storms well short of hurricane strength. Add in the area's humidity and frequent afternoon thunderstorms, and an unprotected opening can let in enough water to start drywall and framing damage before the next inspection even happens.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Panama City

We start by assessing structural safety, a broken window is straightforward, but a compromised roof deck or a wall opened up by fallen debris needs a different approach before anyone climbs a ladder. For window and door openings, we measure, cut, and fasten exterior-grade plywood sized to the opening, not just nailed over the frame, so the seal holds against wind-driven rain. For roofs, we use reinforced poly tarping rated for sustained wind exposure, fastened with batten strips rather than loose nails that pull free in the next gust. On commercial properties we coordinate with property managers so board-up doesn't block required exits or fire access. Every job gets photographed before and after for your insurance file, since board-up and tarping almost always precede a larger restoration claim.

## Panama City Insurance Coordination

Most board-up and tarping calls here follow a storm event, which means an adjuster is often already involved or about to be. We document the damage, the materials used, and the condition of the structure at time of service, which carries weight when a claim later covers roof replacement or interior water damage tied back to the original opening. Coastal and near-coastal policies in Bay County sometimes carry separate wind or named-storm deductibles, so having clear, dated documentation of the emergency work matters more here than it does inland. ## Local Note

One thing that catches people off guard here: Panama City's humidity doesn't pause for storm cleanup. A tarp that's even slightly loose at the edges can trap moist air underneath it, and we've found roof decking that looked fine from the ground but was already softening at the seams within 48 hours of a poor tarp job from someone in a hurry. We seal edges tight and check for ventilation gaps rather than just covering the hole, because in this climate a rushed tarp can create a second problem while it's solving the first.
