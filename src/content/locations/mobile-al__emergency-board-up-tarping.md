## Why Mobile Properties See Board-Up & Tarping Emergencies

Mobile sits low on the bay with a long hurricane season that regularly pushes tropical systems and strong thunderstorm cells through the area from June into November. Mature live oaks and pines common across the city's older residential blocks drop large limbs in straight-line winds, and that kind of impact rarely leaves a clean hole. Roof decking splinters, soffits pull away, and a single broken window can let in enough driving rain to soak insulation and drywall within an hour. Homes built before the 1970s, which make up a good share of Mobile's housing stock, often have original wood roof sheathing that handles impact worse than modern OSB, so a tree strike here tends to open a wider breach than the same storm would cause further inland.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Mobile

We start by assessing structural safety before anyone climbs a ladder, since wet decking and a storm-loosened roofline can hide a fall hazard under what looks like a simple patch job. Openings get measured and covered with exterior-grade plywood sized to the actual stud or rafter bay, not just the hole, because undersized board-up fails first in the next gust. Roof breaches are covered with reinforced poly sheeting rated for wind load, battened down with furring strips rather than loose nails, which matters in a city where a second or third named storm can follow the first within weeks. Every board-up and tarp job is photographed before and after for your insurance file, and our crews follow IICRC guidance for temporary structural protection so the work holds until permanent repairs begin.

## Mobile Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most homeowner and commercial policies along the Gulf Coast include a duty to mitigate further damage after a covered loss, and a properly documented board-up or tarp is usually what satisfies that clause. We provide the photo and material documentation adjusters ask for, and our crews are licensed and insured under license #CBC1253966, which matters when a carrier or a condo association requires proof before authorizing emergency repair work. For properties in deed-restricted neighborhoods, we coordinate the timeline of visible tarping with property management where notice is required, so the temporary fix does not become a separate compliance headache on top of the storm damage.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Board-Up & Tarping

We carry exterior plywood in common thicknesses, UV-resistant tarping rated for sustained wind rather than light-duty poly sheeting, and fastening hardware sized for both wood and masonry substrates, since Mobile's building stock ranges from wood-frame bungalows to brick commercial storefronts downtown. Window openings get a weather-sealed board with foam gasket backing to cut wind infiltration, and roof tarps are run past the damaged area and anchored to sound decking on all sides rather than just taped at the edges, which is where cheaper temporary fixes tend to fail in a gusty afternoon squall.

## Local note

Humidity in Mobile rarely drops below the point where a hastily boarded opening traps moisture against wet framing, so our crews leave small ventilation gaps at the base of tarped roof sections when the structure underneath is already saturated. Skipping that step on a sealed-tight board-up is a fast way to turn a storm hole into a mold problem before the roofer even shows up.
