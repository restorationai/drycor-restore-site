**A storm, falling tree, or house fire just opened up your Lynn Haven roof or windows to the weather?** DRYCOR RESTORE answers 24/7 and gets plywood and tarp on the exposed openings before rain, humidity, or a second storm cell turns a repair into a total loss. Lynn Haven sits in the part of Bay County that took a direct hit from Hurricane Michael, and that history shaped how we approach every board-up and tarping call here: speed first, then a secure structure that holds until permanent repairs or an adjuster inspection happen.

## Why Lynn Haven Properties Need Fast Board-Up and Tarping

Bay County's hurricane season runs long, and Lynn Haven's mix of older ranch-style homes from the 1960s through 1980s alongside newer post-Michael rebuilds means two very different structures are exposed to the same Gulf-driven weather. Older homes often have original roof decking that was never rated for the wind loads Florida's building code now requires, so a missing section of shingles can expose plywood sheathing that wasn't built to shed water quickly. Newer construction with metal roofing and impact windows handles wind better but still needs a tarp over a torn flashing seam or a board over a window broken by storm debris. Either way, every hour a structure sits open to Gulf Coast humidity is an hour mold has to start working on wet insulation and drywall.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Lynn Haven

We start with a structural walk of the exposed area, checking rafters and window framing for damage before anything gets nailed down, since a board fastened to compromised framing won't hold through the next wind event. For roof openings, we use reinforced poly tarping rated for sustained wind, anchored with batten strips rather than just nails through the tarp face, because a loose tarp corner in a Panhandle gust can tear the whole sheet loose by morning. For broken windows and door openings, typically after fire damage or impact from storm debris, we board with exterior-grade plywood sized and sealed to resist wind-driven rain rather than just cut to fit the opening. Everything gets photographed before and after for your insurance file, and documented per industry drying and loss standards so there's no dispute later about what was secured and when.

## Lynn Haven Insurance Coordination

Most Bay County carriers expect emergency mitigation, including board-up and tarping, as a condition of coverage: insurers generally require the property owner to prevent further damage once a loss occurs, and board-up work is the clearest way to show that was done. We photograph the damage, the materials used, and the completed work, then bill most major carriers directly for the mitigation portion so you're not paying out of pocket while a larger claim for roof or fire repair works through your adjuster. If your home is part of an HOA-governed subdivision, we'll coordinate materials and placement with any community exterior guidelines before we leave the site, since even an emergency tarp can draw a compliance notice if it's bright blue against a muted roofline.

## Local note

Lynn Haven homes rebuilt after Hurricane Michael often have hip roofs with steeper pitches than the original ranch stock they replaced, which changes how a tarp has to be rigged. A flatter legacy roof lets us run a tarp straight across and batten the edges; a steeper post-Michael hip roof needs the tarp cut and seamed around the ridge line or wind gets underneath it within a day. We carry both standard and cut tarps on every Bay County call for exactly that reason.
