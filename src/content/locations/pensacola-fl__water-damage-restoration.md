Pensacola's combination of sandy soil, a shallow water table, and a long hurricane season creates water intrusion patterns that look different from drier parts of the state. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Pensacola

We start every call with moisture mapping, not guesswork, because standing water under vinyl plank or engineered hardwood can look dry on the surface while the subfloor stays saturated for days. Extraction comes first, using truck-mounted vacuums for anything beyond a few inches of standing water, followed by targeted pumping in crawlspaces where Pensacola's high water table keeps feeding moisture back in even after the initial source is capped. From there we set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the humidity load typical of a Gulf Coast summer, since standard drying charts built for drier regions tend to underestimate how long Pensacola air holds moisture in wall cavities. Antimicrobial treatment follows on any porous material that sat wet more than 24 to 48 hours, which is often enough time for mold to start colonizing in this climate.
