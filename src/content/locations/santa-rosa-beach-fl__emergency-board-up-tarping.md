## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Santa Rosa Beach

We assess the opening, measure for plywood or heavy-gauge tarp material, and secure the covering to structural framing rather than just trim or siding, since coastal wind loads can tear a poorly anchored tarp loose within hours. For roof work, we run tarps past the damaged area and fasten them with batten strips screwed into rafters or decking, not just staples, because salt-laden air corrodes lighter fasteners faster than it would inland. Window and door openings get exterior-grade plywood cut to size and secured with lag bolts where framing allows, which holds up to repeated wind events better than nailed sheathing. Every job is documented with photos before and after for your insurance file.
