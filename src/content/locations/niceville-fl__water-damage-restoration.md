## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Niceville

We start with extraction, pulling standing water out with truck-mounted or portable pumps before it has a chance to migrate further under baseboards or engineered flooring, which is common in newer Niceville construction and doesn't tolerate prolonged moisture the way solid hardwood does. We document moisture readings daily until materials return to a dry standard, not just until they look dry to the eye.
