## Why Venice Properties See Emergency Plumbing Issues

Much of Venice's housing stock dates to the 1950s through 1980s, when galvanized steel and cast iron were the standard for supply and drain lines. Florida's warm, humid climate and mineral-heavy groundwater accelerate corrosion in those materials far faster than they would in a drier northern climate, and after six or seven decades, pinhole leaks and full pipe failures become routine rather than rare. Homes closer to the Gulf and the area's canals face an added factor: saltwater intrusion into the water table can speed corrosion on copper and galvanized fixtures even further.
