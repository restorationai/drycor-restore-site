## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Carrollwood

We start with extraction using truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on how much standing water is involved, then move to moisture mapping with penetrating and non-penetrating meters so we know exactly how far the water migrated under cabinetry, baseboards, and flooring. In slab homes, that often means checking rooms well outside the visible wet area before we're confident the loss is contained. From there we set structural drying equipment, air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the humidity load, not just the square footage, because Florida's ambient humidity works against standard drying math if you don't compensate for it. We monitor daily with moisture logs until materials read dry per the IICRC S500 standard, not just dry to the touch.
