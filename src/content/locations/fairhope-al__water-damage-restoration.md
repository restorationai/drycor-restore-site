## Why Fairhope Properties See Water Damage Issues

Baldwin County's humid subtropical climate means Fairhope homes rarely get a true dry-out period between weather events. Gulf moisture keeps ambient humidity high most of the year, and summer through late fall brings tropical storm systems that push wind-driven rain into soffits, window frames, and roof penetrations that would stay sealed in a calmer climate. Add in the area's clay-rich soil, which expands and contracts with rainfall and can shift slab foundations over time, and you get a recipe for slow leaks around plumbing penetrations that go unnoticed until flooring starts to cup or baseboards darken. Older homes closer to the bay often sit on crawlspace foundations original to their construction era, and those crawlspaces trap moisture against floor joists long after a surface leak has been mopped up.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Fairhope

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted or portable units depending on how much square footage is affected.
