## Why Valrico Properties See Emergency Plumbing Issues

Most homes in this part of Hillsborough County were built on slab foundations, which means supply and drain lines run under or through concrete rather than in an accessible basement or crawlspace. When a copper or galvanized line corrodes or a joint fails, the first sign is often a warm spot on the floor, a spike in the water bill, or the sound of water running when every fixture is off.
