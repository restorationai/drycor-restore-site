## Why Carrollwood Properties See Board-Up and Tarping Emergencies

Carrollwood sits in unincorporated Hillsborough County, where the building stock runs heavy on the ranch and split-level homes built as the community grew through the 1960s, 70s, and 80s. Many of those roofs are on their second or third shingle layer, and older roof decking doesn't always hold up when a storm tears off a section. The Tampa Bay region also sees some of the most frequent lightning and afternoon thunderstorm activity in the country, which means wind-driven limb strikes and sudden roof punctures aren't rare events here, they're a seasonal pattern. Add in hurricane season from June through November, and a tarp or board-up is often the only thing standing between a damaged exterior and a much larger interior loss.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process

We start with a structural walk-around to confirm what's actually compromised, whether that's a blown-out window from storm debris, a door forced during a break-in, or a roof section stripped by wind. Broken glass and damaged frames get boarded with exterior-grade plywood cut to the opening and fastened into sound framing, not just the damaged trim. Roof damage gets a reinforced tarp system, stretched taut and fastened through furring strips so it doesn't flap loose in the next gust off the bay. Every board-up and tarp job gets photographed before and after, which matters later when the claim gets reviewed.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Board-Up & Tarping

We carry pressure-treated plywood in multiple thicknesses, construction-grade poly and reinforced tarping material rated for sustained wind exposure, and the fasteners and furring strips needed to anchor into tile, shingle, or flat roof substrates without creating new penetration points. For fire-damaged structures we board around compromised framing carefully, since structural members weakened by heat don't always hold fasteners the way sound lumber does. All work follows OSHA-trained safety practices, and where lead-based materials are suspected in older homes, our EPA Lead-Safe Certified crew handles the work accordingly.
