## Why Mobile Properties See Fire Damage Complications

Gulf Coast humidity is the quiet second disaster after any fire in Mobile. Soot is acidic, and in a climate that routinely sits above 70% relative humidity for much of the year, that soot bonds to metal, glass, and painted surfaces faster than it would in a drier region. Chrome fixtures and appliance finishes can pit or tarnish within hours instead of days. Mobile's housing stock adds another layer: a mix of older wood-frame homes, some dating to the early and mid-20th century, alongside newer construction built to post-storm wind codes. Older homes often have heart pine or cypress framing and trim that absorb smoke odor deep into the wood grain, which means surface cleaning alone won't solve the smell. Add in the region's hurricane season, when a fire can follow or precede storm damage to the same structure, and you get properties dealing with compounding issues rather than a single clean loss.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Mobile

We start with a damage assessment that separates heat damage from smoke travel and water used in suppression, since Mobile's humid air means moisture left behind by firefighting efforts can turn into a secondary mold problem within 24 to 48 hours if it's not addressed alongside the fire cleanup. Board-up and tarping come first if the structure is exposed, followed by water extraction where hoses or sprinklers left standing moisture in flooring or wall cavities. From there we work through soot and residue removal calibrated to the surface: dry sponging for porous plaster and drywall, wet cleaning for washable surfaces, and specialized treatment for the heart pine and cypress trim common in older Mobile homes, since those species hold odor longer than modern softwoods. Odor control uses thermal fogging or ozone treatment depending on how deeply smoke penetrated the structure, and we document every step for your insurance file as we go, not after the fact.

## Mobile Insurance Coordination

Fire claims in Mobile often run alongside wind or water claims when a storm is involved, and carriers want clear separation between what the fire caused and what a prior storm event caused. We photograph and log damage by cause and location so your adjuster isn't left guessing which peril affected which part of the structure. Mobile's older housing stock sometimes triggers code-upgrade requirements during repair, since bringing electrical or plumbing up to current code during reconstruction can be a covered cost under some policies but not others. We flag those items early so you and your adjuster can address them before work stalls partway through.
