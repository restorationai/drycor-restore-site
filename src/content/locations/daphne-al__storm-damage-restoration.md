**Storm damage emergency in Daphne?** We answer 24/7, so a call now gets a crew moving before standing water or a breached roof turns a repair into a reconstruction. Daphne sits on the Eastern Shore of Mobile Bay, which means storm systems here bring a mix of straight-line wind, tornado spin-up, and tidal surge that most inland Alabama towns never see, and that combination is exactly where storm damage restoration gets complicated fast.

## Why Daphne Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Baldwin County's position on Mobile Bay puts Daphne directly in the path of tropical systems moving up from the Gulf, and the same storms that bring hurricane-force gusts often stall long enough to dump hours of rain on already-saturated clay soil. That soil doesn't drain quickly, so water backs up against foundations and crawlspaces well after the wind has died down. Mature pine and live oak canopy, common across the older sections of the city, also means tree damage cleanup is rarely a simple matter of clearing a downed limb. Root systems shallow in that same clay soil let whole trees go over, and a tree through a roofline during a tropical storm can open a house to wind-driven rain for hours before anyone notices the ceiling is sagging.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Daphne

The first visit is about stopping the damage from spreading, not starting the full repair. That usually means emergency board-up and tarping on any roof or window breach, followed by extraction if wind-driven rain has gotten into wall cavities or flooring. Because Gulf Coast humidity sits high for most of the year, drying equipment stays on site longer here than it would in a drier climate, and we monitor moisture readings in framing and subfloor rather than guessing when a space looks dry. Once the structure is stabilized, we document everything for the claim and move into reconstruction, whether that's a section of roof decking, drywall that took on wind-driven rain, or a full room rebuild after a tree impact.

## Daphne Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most Daphne homeowners carry wind and hail coverage that's separate from their base homeowner's policy, a holdover from the area's hurricane exposure, and adjusters here are used to seeing claims that involve both wind damage and water intrusion on the same loss. We photograph and document damage in a way that supports that split coverage rather than forcing it into one category. For homes inside one of Daphne's newer platted subdivisions, exterior repairs like roofing color, fencing, and siding often need to match community standards before a board will sign off, and we build that review time into the repair timeline so it doesn't catch anyone off guard mid-claim.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Storm Damage

Standing water after a storm in this part of Baldwin County isn't always clean, since storm surge and heavy runoff can carry bay water, fertilizer runoff, or septic overflow into a structure. We treat that water as contaminated until we know otherwise and bring in extraction and antimicrobial treatment accordingly, rather than drying it out the way we would a simple roof leak. Air movers and dehumidifiers get sized to the humidity load, not just the square footage, because a unit calibrated for a drier climate will underperform here and leave materials damp longer than the readings suggest.

## Local note

Homes built in Daphne before the mid-2000s wind codes tightened often have roof decking attached with fewer nails per sheet than current code requires, which means a storm that only strips shingles on a newer roof can pull decking loose entirely on an older one. We check decking fastening as part of the initial damage assessment on older homes, because a roof that looks like a shingle repair on the surface sometimes needs sheathing replaced underneath before a new roof can go on correctly.
