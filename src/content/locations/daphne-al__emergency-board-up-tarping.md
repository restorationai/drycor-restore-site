**Roof torn open or a window blown out in Daphne?** Call now, we answer 24/7 to get a tarp or board in place before the next band of rain moves through. Emergency board-up and tarping is about buying time, sealing the structure against wind-driven rain, looters, and curious wildlife until permanent repairs or a full restoration scope can begin. On the Eastern Shore of Mobile Bay, that window of exposure matters more than most places, because the next storm cell is rarely far behind.

## Why Daphne Properties Need Board-Up & Tarping

Daphne sits in Baldwin County on the Eastern Shore of Mobile Bay, which means it takes the brunt of Gulf storm tracks from June through November, plus the straight-line wind and hail that come with inland thunderstorm lines the rest of the year. Mature live oaks and pines shade a lot of the older lots here, and a snapped limb through a roof deck or a picture window is one of the most common calls we get after a severe weather pass. Homes closer to the bay also take salt-laden wind that accelerates rot in exposed soffit and fascia, so a hole left unprotected for even a few days can let moisture into framing that was already working against humidity.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Daphne

We start with a structural walk of the damage, photographing everything for the insurance file before anything gets covered. For roof openings, we use reinforced poly sheeting rated for wind load, fastened with batten strips rather than just nails through the tarp, because a flapping tarp in a Gulf breeze will tear itself loose within a day. Window and door openings get exterior-grade plywood cut to the actual opening (not a generic sheet), sealed at the edges to keep wind-driven rain from wicking behind the board. Everything is installed to hold through a follow-on storm, not just overnight, since Baldwin County's wind-load expectations are higher than what you'd see further inland.

## Daphne Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most Daphne homeowners' policies treat emergency board-up and tarping as a required mitigation step, meaning the carrier expects it and typically reimburses it as part of the claim rather than treating it as optional. We document the pre-existing damage and the temporary repair itself so there's a clear record for your adjuster. If your property is in one of Daphne's newer platted subdivisions with an active HOA, exterior board-up materials sometimes need to match a color or finish standard before permanent repairs begin; we can hold to a neutral, low-profile install until that's sorted out so you're not fighting two problems at once.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Board-Up & Tarping

We carry marine-grade tarps rather than standard poly sheeting for bay-adjacent properties, since standard tarps degrade faster under salt air and UV exposure. Plywood is treated or primed where it will sit against open framing for more than a few days, which matters here given how quickly humidity sets in once a structure is open to outside air. For larger roof sections we use batten-and-screw securement patterns designed to resist sustained wind rather than just staple guns, which is a meaningful difference during active storm season.

## Local note

Daphne is known for its late-summer "jubilees," when oxygen-depleted water along Mobile Bay pushes fish and shellfish into shallow water, usually during the same hot, still, humid stretches that precede fast-moving severe thunderstorm lines.
